# 🌍 TERRAFUSE

### **The AI Environmental Decision Firewall**

> **Before an organization makes a decision that affects the environment, TerraFuse calculates the hidden environmental cost, verifies the evidence, finds lower-impact alternatives, and produces an auditable action plan.**

Think:

**Grant Guardian → but for environmental decisions.**

Not an environmental information website.

Not another carbon calculator.

Not another recycling app.

It's an **agentic decision system**.

---

# 🔥 THE CORE IDEA

Imagine a college, restaurant, small company, event organizer, school, or municipality wants to buy something.

Example:

> "We need 10,000 disposable food containers for our college festival."

Normally:

**Purchase → money spent → waste generated**

TerraFuse intervenes **before the decision**.

You upload:

-  procurement request 
-  invoice/quote 
-  product specification 
-  PDF 
-  spreadsheet 
-  vendor proposal 

TerraFuse analyzes it.

Then it produces:

### ENVIRONMENTAL DECISION

**Current proposal**

10,000 single-use containers

Estimated:

-  🌳 material impact 
-  💧 water footprint 
-  ⚡ energy impact 
-  🗑️ waste generation 
-  🌡️ estimated CO₂e 
-  ♻️ recyclability 
-  🚚 transportation impact 

Then:

### ALTERNATIVE A

Reusable containers

### ALTERNATIVE B

Compostable containers

### ALTERNATIVE C

Recycled-content containers

And the system doesn't just say:

> "Alternative B is greener."

It explains **why**, cites the evidence, shows assumptions, and gives the decision-maker a recommendation.

# 🌡️ AGENT 2 — Carbon Agent

Calculates an estimated emissions footprint.

But here's the important part:

**Don't let the LLM invent the number.**

Use:

```
```

```
LLM
 ↓
identify material/product
 ↓
structured parameters
 ↓
deterministic calculation
 ↓
result
```

The LLM interprets.

The calculation engine calculates.

That gives you a much more defensible architecture.

---

# 💧 AGENT 3 — Water Agent

Estimate water implications where credible data exists.

Again:

**LLM ≠ calculator.**

Use structured factors/data sources.
